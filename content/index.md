I'm Flaky, an autistic/ADHD blue jay from the UK, mainly doing projects focused on music metadata, such as:

- [mb_MusicBrainzSync](https://github.com/FlakyBlueJay/mb_musicbrainzsync), a plugin for sending user tags/ratings to MusicBrainz via MusicBee
- The [RateYourMusic tag hierarchy template](https://github.com/FlakyBlueJay/mb-rym-hierarchy) for MusicBee based on Rate Your Music's genre, descriptor, and scenes & movements trees
- The [Tag Hierarchy Manager](https://github.com/FlakyBlueJay/TagHierarchyManager) for managing complex tag hierarchy templates

Some of my hobbies include tech and music, with a hyperfixation on the Eurovision Song Contest that I'm trying to shift, to no avail.

You can find me on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/flaky.blue), [Mastodon](https://furry.engineer/@Flaky), [Rate Your Music](https://rateyourmusic.com/~flaky_bastard), [Last.fm](https://www.last.fm/user/DonWada), and [GitHub](https://github.com/FlakyBlueJay).
