Essentially the genre, scenes & movements and descriptor trees ported to a tag hierarchy template you can add into MusicBee. I use RYM quite a lot, including for genre metadata, so I figured I'd port over the RYM trees to a tag hierarchy template.

[Available on GitHub](https://github.com/FlakyBlueJay/mb-rym-hierarchy)
