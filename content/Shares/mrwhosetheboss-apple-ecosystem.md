title: I Escaped the Apple Ecosystem - It Backfired (mrwhosetheboss)
---

![](https://youtu.be/lE_11_paGoA)

Pretty good video explaining how moving away the Apple ecosystem is not that frictionless, especially when there are things that can be considered dealbreakers. I relate a lot to how bad it was for Arun to go for a non-Apple smartwatch, as my Apple Watch has been *immensely* helpful for dealing with the worst parts of my ADHD to the point it's a necessity, and it doesn't seem like *any* company is doing it right besides Apple. (he touches on issues with Garmin, Google, Samsung, and even Oppo, whose phone he actually loved for the better cameras and is one of the drivers for this video)

See also: [Apple Watch or Don't Bother (fireborn.mataora.blog)](https://fireborn.mataroa.blog/blog/apple-watch-or-dont-bother/) - this person is blind and used to use Linux, but stopped after it was clear it wasn't going to be accessible. This is one of their posts about Apple.
