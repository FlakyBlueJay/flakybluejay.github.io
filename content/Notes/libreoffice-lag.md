I *want* to keep using LibreOffice (specifically Calc, its spreadsheet app), but the constant hitching and lag on Windows is annoying enough that I've had to move to OnlyOffice. Furthermore, and I don't know if this is LibreOffice or a limitation of the <abbr title="OpenDocument Spreadsheet">ODS</abbr> format, but if you try to set a conditional formatting formula's range, it just sets it to an absurdly large range instead of just setting it to the entire column. It makes the range formula look unwieldy especially when the conditional format stops working.

OnlyOffice is much smoother, and has stronger compatibility with Microsoft Office files. It's a no-brainer for me at this point, besides doing the obvious and getting Microsoft Office.
